# SMAR-2652 Test Cases

## Test Case Coverage Summary

| Group | Coverage | Priority Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Group 1 | Add-step prerequisite, dynamic validation, removed Generate Asset dependency, save and persistence | High |
| Group 2 | Protected prerequisite deletion and independent deletion of asset steps | High |
| Group 3 | Invalid dependency reordering and independent Generate Asset reorder regression | High and Medium |
