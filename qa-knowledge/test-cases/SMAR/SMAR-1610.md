## Duplicate Review

- Existing related cases checked: SMAR-2638, SMAR-2656, and SMAR-2658 dispatcher/alarm/client-file coverage.
- Overlap found: dispatcher availability, alarm processing, and Portal client workflow regression.
- Decision: retain the SMAR-1610 cases because their primary goal is removal of stored `ClientFile.DeviceId`, current assignment resolution, unknown-alarm routing, or Portal primary-dialler selection; authentication/deprecation cases do not verify those outcomes.
- Within this suite, G1-09/G1-10 and G2-01/G2-02 use similar Call actions but verify distinct entry-point guards versus assignment lifecycle outcomes.

## Shared Execution Controls

- Use synthetic non-production Client Files, diallers, mobile devices, login identifiers, alarm payloads, and unique correlation IDs.
- Confirm the target build contains SMAR-1610 and record the CP Desktop, Portal Web, Old SHM, and alarm-service versions.
- Capture baseline device/file relationships and alarm counts before mutation.
- Restore reassigned devices after each case and remove only controlled alarm records where approved while preserving required audit evidence.
- Developer- or operations-supported evidence is required for serialized contracts, relationship storage, handler/job correlation, and duplicate-processing checks that testers cannot access directly.
