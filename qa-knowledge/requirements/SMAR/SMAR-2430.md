## Requirement Summary

SMAR-2430 changes old-backend processing so an incoming `AlarmCancelled` event is not automatically closed and hidden from nurses. The backend converts it to Alarm Type `Emergency`, sets `Restoral = True`, sets Alarm Details to `Restoral of Emergency Alarm`, stores the alarm, and makes it visible in CP Desktop for nurse action. When a matching Emergency exists, the Restoral is displayed as its child. Verified Confluence behaviour also covers a closed original Emergency, no received original Emergency, duplicate cancellation events, child-only closure, history evidence, and correct client isolation.

## Confirmed Scope

- Old-backend receipt and conversion of `AlarmCancelled` from an applicable connected device/IPD flow.
- Emergency alarm persistence and visibility in CP Desktop.
- Emergency Restoral details, alarm history, correct client/device association, client grouping, and parent-child placement.
- Authorized nurse action and child-only closure behaviour.
- Closed original Emergency, no received original Emergency, repeated cancellation delivery, and multiple-client isolation.

## Out of Scope

- Mobile application process changes covered by MA-2150.
- Restoral processing for alarm types other than the SMAR-2430 `AlarmCancelled` to Emergency conversion.
- New FCM, SMS, email, Twilio, escalation, or external-recipient behaviour; no such change is stated by SMAR-2430.
- Production execution with real clients, contacts, or emergency services.

## Business Rules

1. A received `AlarmCancelled` is converted to a visible Emergency Restoral so a nurse can identify and handle the cancellation.
2. A matching original Emergency makes the Restoral its child in the same client group.
3. Absence or prior closure of the original Emergency must not suppress the Restoral.
4. Duplicate delivery must not create duplicate Restoral alarms.
5. Closing a Restoral child must not close its parent or sibling alarms.
6. Client and device associations must not cross client-file boundaries.

## UI/UX Rules

- The Emergency Restoral is visible on the CP Desktop alarm screen.
- Alarm Details display exact red text `Restoral of Emergency Alarm`.
- When an original Emergency exists, the Restoral is visible beneath it as a child in the same client group.
- The nurse can open and action the Restoral through the supported alarm workflow.

## Validation Rules

- A repeated identical `AlarmCancelled` delivery produces one Restoral outcome.
- A missing or closed original Emergency does not cause the cancellation to be ignored.
- Multiple-client alarm data must remain isolated to the event's correct client and device.

## API / Backend Impact

## Regression Risk

Alarm ingestion, CP alarm visibility, client/device matching, parent-child grouping, nurse action/closure, alarm history, and existing Emergency handling share the affected flow. Regression must confirm that normal Emergency alarms remain visible and actionable, a child close does not close its parent or siblings, duplicate cancellation delivery creates one Restoral, a closed or absent parent does not suppress a Restoral, and one client's event never appears under another client's alarm group.

## Confirmed Decisions
