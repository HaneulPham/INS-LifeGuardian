## Duplicate Review

- Existing related cases checked: SMAR-2602 Twilio/GetDeviceByLineNumber route selection, SMAR-2638 dispatcher authentication/alarm evidence, and SMAR-2659 SafetyWatch asset identity.
- Duplicate coverage found: No exact duplicate.
- Overlap explanation: SMAR-2602 verifies Primary/Failover routing; SMAR-2404 verifies device-type-specific LineNumber/Barcode mapping and final alarm association.
- Decision: Keep distinct request, transformer/UI, and Twilio cases because their evidence and final outcomes differ.
- No source case was merged or removed.

## Shared Execution Controls

- Use isolated non-production SafetyWatch and SmartHomeMini/Mobile devices, client files, lookup values, alarms, and credentials.
- Keep API keys and Twilio authorization in approved environment variables; never store their values in evidence or this file.
- Use unique LineNumbers and Barcodes that do not belong to another device. Record DeviceUuid and client-file association before execution.
- Record the deployed dispatcher accepted/not-found status and body because SMAR-2404 does not define exact HTTP contracts.
- Alarm cases require a baseline alarm count and cleanup that removes only controlled test alarms while preserving required audit/security evidence.
