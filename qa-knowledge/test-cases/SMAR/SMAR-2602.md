## Duplicate Review

- Existing related test cases checked: SMAR-2635, SMAR-2638, SMAR-2650, SMAR-2656, and SMAR-2658.
- Partial workflow overlap exists for Mobile account cancellation, alarms, HMAC-protected legacy calls, and backend failure integrity.
- Decision: retain all 30 approved SMAR-2602 IDs. Each case uniquely maps one of 15 affected InsService/InsSmsService requests to one of the two confirmed routing states; related tickets do not verify `INSFailover` selection or Primary/Failover non-contact.
- No cases were merged or removed; no duplicate titles or IDs are introduced.

## Shared Execution Controls

- Execute only in Staging with synthetic or controlled clients, devices, files, alarms, reports, groups, accounts, and recipients.
- Record the `INSFailover` Parameter Store value and last-modified timestamp before each group; never store URL values, credentials, tokens, phone numbers, client identities, or tenant identifiers in evidence.
- Isolate alarm, SMS, email, report, and follow-up recipients from production carers, nurses, emergency services, clients, and contacts.
- Capture before/after business state, execution time, and a safe correlation identifier where supported.
- Restore the approved Staging setting after execution and verify cleanup affects only disposable test data; retain required sanitized audit and routing evidence.
